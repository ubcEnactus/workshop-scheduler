# Workshop Scheduler: admin guide

Prepared 27 September 2026. For the public Ennovate and Enspire admin demos.

## 1. Start here

Open your program's link supplied with this guide. No Vercel account is needed.

- **Your own account:** enter the email your admin added and choose **Send sign-in link**. Open the email to reach your admin, PA or teacher dashboard. No password is needed. If email sign-in is marked unavailable, the app owner still needs to activate email delivery.
- **Easy demo access:** choose **Continue as demo admin** or **Continue as demo PA**. These shared accounts send no email. Anyone visiting can use them and access the same saved workspace.

Ennovate and Enspire have separate workspaces. They began without sample schools, teachers or bookings; each now has shared demo admin and PA accounts. Changes are saved. Sessions last two hours, but signing out or expiry does **not** delete saved information. Use rehearsal data while public demo access is enabled.

**The everyday flow: set up schools and teachers, then Plan → Staff → Publish.**

- A **workshop** is one named delivery with a date range, such as your October delivery.
- A **class session** is one class's actual date, time and PA team within that workshop.
- A **draft** is private to admins. **Published** means the session is official and available in participant views.
- **PA** means program assistant. All dates and times shown in the app use Vancouver time.

### Your first rehearsal: about 15 minutes

1. In **Schools & teachers**, add a school and a teacher at that school. Each teacher represents their class.
2. Open the teacher’s **Availability** and record one teacher-confirmed weekly time, including when it starts to apply.
3. For a quick rehearsal, use **Demo PA** in the PAs list. To try PA availability, sign out, choose **Continue as demo PA**, enter a weekly time in **My availability**, then return through **Continue as demo admin**.
4. In **Workshops → Create a workshop**, enter a name, a future delivery window and the staffing requirement. For a small rehearsal, a minimum of one PA is enough. Add the teacher.
5. In **Plan**, select a date and time. Click **Save dates & continue**.
6. In **Staff**, assign the PA. If availability is missing, expect a warning and confirm the time outside the app before assigning a real person. Assigning saves immediately to the private draft.
7. Open **Publish**, review the session, select it and click **Publish 1 session**. Find it in **Calendar** afterward.

Demo buttons and publishing send no email. Adding a person saves their invited email address; it does not send an invitation or add an easy-access demo button. Once email delivery is activated, that person can request their own sign-in link.

Admins can add another coordinator from **Admins → Add admin**. The new admin receives full scheduling and account-management access, so add only trusted coordinators. Adding the account does not send an invitation; share the normal sign-in page so they can request a one-time link.

## 2. Set up people and availability

### Schools and teachers

Start in **Schools & teachers**. Use **Schools** to add each school by name, then **Add teacher** to save the teacher’s name, email and school. The teacher’s availability page opens immediately; no separate class setup is needed.

Open the teacher’s **Availability**. Under **Weekly availability**, enter the weekday, start and end time, and effective dates provided by the teacher. Use **Save weekly time**. Add a **date-specific change** for a one-off closure or extra time; record a school closure when it affects the whole school.

Availability describes possible times. It does not book a visit. If an older block is marked as a reference, activate it before expecting date suggestions.

For two classes attending together, pick one representative class and describe the combined group in its internal note. Include that representative class once in the workshop.

### PAs

Open **PAs → Add PA** and enter the person's name and email. **Availability & workload** lets admins review submitted availability and existing commitments.

PAs enter their own recurring times in **My availability**, with effective dates and one-off exceptions. Teachers provide their class times to admins outside the app. Teachers cannot change the schedule; PAs cannot accept, decline or edit assignments.

To rehearse the PA workflow, sign in with **Continue as demo PA**. Personal PA accounts use their invited email once email delivery is activated. Manual assignment remains available when availability is missing; a warning does not mean the person has confirmed they can attend.

### Create the workshop

Go to **Workshops → Create a workshop**. Give this delivery a clear name, its first and last possible dates, usual duration, and minimum/maximum PA numbers. Both ends of the delivery window are included. Select **Add teachers** and add the participating classes.

Each class gets one active session in that workshop. For another visit, create another workshop. A class is not automatically included just because it exists in the directory, and changing the calendar month does not create any bookings.

## 3. Plan dates and staff the sessions

### Plan

1. Open your workshop and choose **1. Plan**.
2. Select one date and time for each class you are ready to schedule. Use **Change week** to see later options. Check duration, location and participant instructions.
3. Review the PA availability information. The combined check estimates whether the selected sessions can be staffed together; it does not assign anyone.
4. Choose **Save dates & continue** to save private sessions and open Staff. **Save dates** keeps you in Plan. You can save some classes now and finish the rest later.

If no dates appear, check class enrollment, the teacher contact, weekly availability and effective dates, closures, and the workshop's delivery window. If a teacher has already confirmed a specific time, use **Schedule confirmed session** from Schools & teachers.

### Staff

Select the dated drafts you want to work on, then use **Auto-fill missing PAs**. It adds only enough eligible PAs to reach the minimum. It keeps existing teams and dates. It may leave gaps when nobody meets the automatic rules.

Automatic assignment needs full-session availability, no overlapping commitments, and normally no other session that day or that Monday-to-Friday week. It prefers suitable PAs with fewer total assignments. There is no required monthly quota setup.

Use **Assign** or **Remove** to change a team yourself. These edits save immediately, while the session remains a private draft. **Undo** is available in recent draft activity; it can refuse if later work or a new conflict makes the reversal unsafe.

### Read the warnings before assigning

- **Missing or partial availability:** confirm that the PA can attend the whole session.
- **Same-day warning, shown in red:** review the other commitment and allow enough time between locations.
- **Same-week warning, shown in amber:** confirm that the additional workload is reasonable.
- **Blocked assignment:** choose a different person or time. Overlapping PA assignments and consecutive classes at the same school cannot be bypassed by a warning approval.

For a warning-only candidate, clicking **Assign** records your decision; there is no extra override form. The warning remains visible. Never treat a filled place as proof that the PA has been contacted.

Removing a PA also excludes them from that session's future auto-fill. Add them manually or choose **Allow auto-fill for [name]** to restore eligibility. If a session shows **Auto-fill off**, open **Session options → Allow auto-fill** when you want automatic additions again.

## 4. Publish, communicate and handle changes

### Publish deliberately

Open **3. Publish**. Check the class, date, time, location, PA names and all warnings. Select only the ready sessions you want to make official, then click **Publish [number] sessions**. Other drafts remain private. A sufficient headcount alone may not resolve all readiness problems.

Publication makes the session visible to assigned PAs and teachers at the school when those people have access. **It sends no email or message.** Contact the school and assigned PAs yourself, then use **Record communication** for that change. The communication checklist records your follow-up; it does not send it.

Keep arrival instructions and meeting links in participant instructions. Keep coordination notes in internal admin notes. Teachers see published sessions for their school; PAs see their own published assignments.

### When plans change

- **A PA withdraws:** open the session and remove or replace them. A published session can remain published with a visible staffing gap while you find a replacement. Tell the affected people.
- **The time, location or team changes:** open the session's edit controls. Review the combined change, apply it, then communicate it. Moving one session outside the workshop window requires an explicit date exception.
- **The visit will not happen:** cancel it and tell the school and PAs. Published cancellation history stays visible. A cancelled session can be replaced later.
- **The visit has happened:** mark it complete after its end time. Recording communication and marking completion are separate tasks.
- **A workshop was created by mistake:** use **Workshop options → Delete workshop** and review the confirmation. Deletion is available only for unused or draft-only workshops. Published, cancelled or completed history prevents deletion.

### If something looks wrong

**A save is uncertain:** use **Retry and check save**. Do not create a second booking to compensate for a save that might already have worked.

**Auto-fill leaves gaps:** review PA availability and commitments; assign a confirmed PA manually, choose another date, or keep the session as a draft. Auto-fill does not move dates.

**Your session is missing:** check the workshop, calendar month and filters. If a batch is selected, use **Show full workshop**. If the sign-in has expired, sign in again.

**An error keeps returning:** send the app owner the workshop name, what you clicked, when it happened, and the visible error message or reference. Avoid repeatedly making the same change.

Before ending an admin session, check remaining staffing gaps, unscheduled classes, unpublished drafts and communication tasks. Agree who owns each follow-up.
